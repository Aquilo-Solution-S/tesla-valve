A sixth kind, pulse, occupies a vertical three-cell column and is unchanged by rotation.
Each advanced random value chooses pulse when its remainder modulo 16 is 15;
otherwise the five existing choices keep their remainder-modulo-5 selection.
On locking it leaves no cells and erases the clipped 3x3 square around its bottom cell.
Its lock award is 11 plus 3 per erased cell, with ordinary drop points and row clearing.
All its precise facts are in section 4 of spec.md.
